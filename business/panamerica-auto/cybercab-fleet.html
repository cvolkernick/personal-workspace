FAQ

Short answers

Can I buy a Cybercab from Panamerica?
No. We do not sell vehicles. Tesla is not taking Cybercab orders. Nobody here can sell you one.
Are you raising money on this page?
No. This is an interest form for a Turo → Cybercab operator path. A real raise would be a separate, counsel-led process with offering documents. That process is not open.
Is Panamerica affiliated with Tesla?
No. We may fill Tesla’s public interest form like any other operator. That is not a partnership.
Why start on Turo if the story is Cybercab?
Because Tesla is not selling units to third parties yet, and SWFL is not a live Robotaxi metro. Turo is the cashflow and the ops school. Cybercab is the option we prepare for.

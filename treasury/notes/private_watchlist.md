## ORANGE JUICE HODLINGS Inc.

- **Status:** Private permanent-capital holding company; not publicly listed.
- **What it does:** Acquires profitable small and mid-sized cash-flowing businesses, improves operations, and intends to hold them indefinitely rather than follow a private-equity buy/flip model. Retained cash flow is intended to fund further acquisitions and a long-term Bitcoin treasury. Initial targets reportedly generate **$1M–$10M of annual cash flow**.
- **Structure / people:** Launched with **$40M** of capital. The founding/partner group includes **Lyn Alden, Jeff Booth, Nico Lechuga, Andi Pitt, Adrian Steckel, and Ruben Zweiban**. Lyn is a partner, founder of Lyn Alden Investment Strategies, and general partner of Ego Death Capital; Ruben Zweiban is the operating partner handling day-to-day operations.
- **Access today:** The article invites interested investors and business sellers to contact the company. No public investment mechanism, fund wrapper, token, or accreditation-only terms are disclosed in the article/press materials; no current public purchase route is stated.
- **Listing:** The company intends to pursue a future public listing to provide liquid ownership currency and capital-markets access, but gives **no timeline**.
- **Fees / size:** Launch capital was **$40M**; no management or performance fees were disclosed.
- **Portfolio note:** **Potential stocks + BTC/digital-credit hybrid candidate**—cash-flowing operating businesses plus an intended Bitcoin treasury. Research/watchlist only; do not treat as a NAV target or order.

**Sources:** Lyn Alden, [“An Alternative to Private Equity: Introducing ORANGE JUICE”](https://share.google/kp2UCgswD8g39fjYV) (July 15, 2026); [ORANGE JUICE](https://www.orangejuice.com) official site and launch materials.

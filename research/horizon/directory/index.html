01

The big picture

You make the calls. Grok turns them into work and hands it to the right specialist. Specialists report back to Grok, and Grok briefs you once. GVG and Buzz work alongside the team through GitHub. Everything runs on prism, your home server.

The one rule

Specialists → Grok → you. Only two things skip Grok: an emergency STOP, and Byline's article drafts for your review.

Quiet by default

Every daily brief is “send only if it matters.” Silence means nothing needs you.

Money is yours

No teammate buys, trades or sends money unless you've ordered it or set up a standing transfer.

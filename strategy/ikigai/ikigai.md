# Ikigai — Layer 0 Identity / North Star

**Role:** Slow-changing identity framework. Everything below (B2 knowledge, Horizon seasonal plans, Orchestrator next actions, domain dashboards) should be *compatible* with this — not a daily rewrite of who you are.

**Machine-readable twin:** [`pillars.json`](./pillars.json) (Orchestrator + agents load this).  
**Thematic bets nest under Ikigai:** see [`../bets.md`](../bets.md) — bets are *vehicles*, not the whole identity.

---

## Center (Ikigai)

Build **personal leverage and financial optionality** by shipping systems (AI/Autonomy) that multiply output, funding freedom through disciplined thematic bets (Energy, Bitcoin, AI), and protecting **embodied energy** (fitness) so deep work stays sustainable.

**Themes:** AI/Autonomy leverage · Wealth optionality · Embodied energy · Systems that compound

---

## Four pillars

### Love
- Building leverage systems and agentic tooling  
- Shipping small automations that remove friction  
- Fitness and recovery as craft  
- Creative / technical construction  

### Good at
- Systems design and personal tooling  
- Multi-domain orchestration and synthesis  
- Engineering dashboards and workflows  
- Turning messy context into structured plans  

### World needs
- Tools that multiply human output and autonomy  
- Energy transition / abundance (attention + capital)  
- Personal systems that reduce chaos and raise optionality  

### Paid for
- Thematic wealth building (Bitcoin, Energy, AI-linked exposure)  
- Skills and systems that increase earning / capital leverage  
- Liquidity and treasury hygiene as optionality infrastructure  

---

## Intersections

| Intersection | Meaning | Focus |
|--------------|---------|--------|
| **Passion** | Love ∩ Good at | Orchestrator, agents, small leverage automations, fitness craft |
| **Mission** | Love ∩ World needs | Autonomy-increasing systems; reusable clarity tools |
| **Profession** | Good at ∩ Paid for | Systems/automation craft; productized thinking |
| **Vocation** | World needs ∩ Paid for | Thematic bets + capital allocation discipline |
| **Center** | All four | Statement above |

## Out of bounds

- Dashboard sprawl without a single next action  
- Hygiene theater that interrupts deep work  
- Exploring every domain when intent asks for focus  
- Treating tools as identity instead of leverage

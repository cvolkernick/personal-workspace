## Cutting Phase Plan (with Intermittent Fasting)

**Target:** 2,000-2,285 cal/day | 200g protein | 200g carbs | 50g fat
**Fasting:** 16:8 (skip breakfast, eat 12pm-8pm)

### Daily Structure

| Meal | Time | Calories | P | C | F | Foods |
|------|------|----------|---|---|---|-------|
| **Lunch (Break Fast)** | 12:00 PM | 600 | 50 | 60 | 15 | Chicken breast (150g), Rice (150g), Veggies |
| **Snack** | 3:00 PM | 300 | 30 | 20 | 5 | Greek yogurt (200g), Banana |
| **Dinner** | 6:30 PM | 500 | 50 | 50 | 10 | Tilapia (150g), Sweet potato (150g), Salad |

### Weekly Meal Prep

- **Sunday:** Cook chicken breasts (1kg), brown rice (500g cooked)
- **Wednesday:** Cook tilapia, sweet potatoes
- **Daily:** Mix up veggies, rotate proteins

### Cutting Shopping List (Weekly)

- Chicken breast: 1.5 kg
- Tilapia: 500g
- Greek yogurt: 1.5 kg
- Brown rice: 1 kg
- Sweet potatoes: 2 kg
- Bananas: 10

## Key Principles

1. **Protein priority:** Hit protein target first at every meal
2. **Carbs around training:** Save biggest carb meals for workout days
3. **Fiber:** Include veggies at lunch and dinner
4. **Water:** 3-4L daily minimum
5. **Prep:** Cook in batches on Sunday/Wednesday

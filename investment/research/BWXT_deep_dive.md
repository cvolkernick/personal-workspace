## Thesis fit

**Northstar:** Energy, including nuclear. Smith's long-cycle winner is the *nuclear cycle* (fuel + hardware), not a gas-producer tape. BWXT is the hardware leg; CCJ is the fuel + 49% OEM leg.

**Bull case (real):**
- Sole-source-class Navy propulsion franchise. Shipbuilding plan *raises* cadence (carriers to 4-year). That is a multi-decade floor, not a data-center narrative.
- Commercial nuclear components are what a US new-build cycle actually buys (steam generators, vessels, fuel handling) — PCG is the US footprint bet.
- Microreactor / TRISO / mPower is real optionality. It is **not** the 2026 P&L (volumes down this quarter). Do not pay BANR/Janus slideware as if it were Columbia-class.

**Why not instead of CCJ:**
- One nuclear name = **CCJ** (named fuel + Westinghouse gap). BWXT is the *second* seat because hardware ≠ pounds. Owner picked A, not B.

**Why not instead of BE:**
- BE is SOFC speed-to-power. BWXT does not ship in 55 days. Different bottleneck. "We need energy" still surfaces BE.

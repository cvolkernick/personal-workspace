3. **Tesla Supercharger white-label is a real circumstance change — not yet a de-risked turnaround.** Aug 2026: EVgo + Tesla agreement to deploy **EVgo-owned and branded V4 Superchargers** (via Supercharger for Business), first installs expected **late 2026**, locations expected on Tesla in-car navigation; Magic Dock / multi-standard access reported in trade press. Does **not** advance Bitcoin, Autonomy, or Robotics. Opportunity cost vs mega-cap AI (GOOGL/NVDA) and BE is high on a ~$177 book.

5. **Owner chart skepticism is justified.** Lower throughput per stall trends, EV sales forecast cuts baked into underwriting, widening Adj. EBITDA losses, and total-revenue volatility are consistent with a **value-trap risk** if Supercharger rollout slips or utilization fails to inflect.

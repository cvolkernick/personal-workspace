## Bull case (Thesis)

1. **AI data-center power is real demand.** FY2025 revenue ~**$2.02B** (record), company attributed growth to AI data center + C&I. Q1’26 revenue **$751.1M** (**+130%** YoY); product revenue **+208%** YoY.
2. **Guidance raised hard.** After Q1’26, FY2026 revenue guide **$3.4B–$3.8B** (~**80%** growth midpoint vs prior ~60%); non-GAAP OI **$600–750M**; non-GAAP EPS **$1.85–$2.25**; non-GAAP GM ~**34%**.
3. **Operating leverage showing up.** Q1’26 GAAP OI **$72.2M** (vs loss YoY); non-GAAP OI **$129.7M**; positive operating cash flow **$73.6M** in Q1’26.
4. **Strategic partners.** Public narrative around multi-GW data-center / fuel-cell agreements (e.g. Oracle expansion, Brookfield AI infra relationships / JVs in filings commentary). Reinforces “go-to on-site power” story.
5. **Balance sheet cash.** ~**$2.49B** cash & equivalents (Mar 31, 2026) supports scale-up—though matched by large **recourse debt** (~**$2.60B**).

**Thesis confidence (bull fundamentals):** ~0.65 — execution and demand look real on company prints; stock price already prices a lot of it.

## Bear case (Risk)

1. **Valuation / multiple risk.** After a huge run, several independent narratives flag **overvaluation** vs fair-value models; high revenue/EBITDA multiples require sustained AI DC conversion. Pullbacks can be violent (beta high).
2. **AI demand is binary for the stock story.** Company risk factors explicitly include **AI adoption slowdown → slower AI data-center expansion**.
3. **Leverage & capital intensity.** ~**$2.6B recourse debt**; large manufacturing / install cycles; project financing; backlog recognition risk.
4. **Customer / related-party concentration.** Material **related-party revenue** in recent quarters (disclosed in Q1 notes)—partner economics and timing matter.
5. **Competition & tech path.** Grid buildout, gas turbines, batteries, nuclear SMRs, other fuel cells / generators all compete for “AI power.” Gas-reliant SOFC optics vs zero-carbon alternatives can reprice the story.
6. **Policy / tax / tariff.** IRA / tax credit / trade policy called out in forward-looking risks.
7. **Book-size mismatch.** Agentic NAV ~**$8** — even a “full” thematic bet is **noise** in risk terms but still **opportunity cost** vs funding core 40/60 names. No edge from a $1–2 starter on a $60B+ name.

**Risk confidence:** ~0.75 that near-term *buy* is poorly skewed for this book.

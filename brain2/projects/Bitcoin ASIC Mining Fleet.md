# Bitcoin ASIC Mining Fleet

## Outcome

Scale a Bitcoin mining ASIC fleet as one of the two **primary capital engines**.

## Current state (high-level)

- **2 ASICs** (count only — no hashes, balances, or site secrets here).
- Unitary model is mastered; remaining work is capital-driven repetition and scaling.

## Success metric

Any additional ASICs = concrete progress. Exploring **Simple Mining** as a lower-capex / turnkey acquisition path.

## Constraints

- **Capital is the binding constraint.**
- Energy / power availability is a supporting theme (see [[Strategy & Bets]]).

## Non-goals (for now)

- Detailed host contracts, live profitability tables, or API dumps in B2.
- Replacing the unitary model with constant experimental pivots.

## Current Kaizen Focus

Improvements judged by capital efficiency and process simplicity per [[Kaizen & Continuous Improvement]].

# Vehicle Rental Management Fleet

## Outcome

Scale a vehicle rental / management fleet (**owned units + asset-light managed client vehicles**) as one of the two **primary capital engines**, with a longer-term path toward Tesla robotaxi / broader autonomous mobility.

## Current state (high-level)

- **2 Turo vehicles**.
- Additional client pipeline possible.
- Actively designing **AI-assisted** mechanisms for locating and onboarding client vehicle owners under management (asset-light growth).

## Success metric

Any additional **owned** vehicles or **managed** (client-owned) units = concrete progress.

## Constraints

- Capital for owned units.
- Pipeline and process for managed owners.
- Systems / AI skills are the leverage point for the managed-owner channel ([[Agents & Tooling]]).

## Non-goals (for now)

- Live occupancy rates, revenue dumps, or owner PII in B2.
- Scope explosion into unrelated side hustles that starve fleet scaling.

## Current Kaizen Focus

AI systems for locating, qualifying, and onboarding client vehicle owners under management (process cycle time, conversion, and automation of steps).  
All improvements follow [[Kaizen & Continuous Improvement]] principles.

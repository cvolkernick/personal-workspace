# Ikigai & Identity

**Layer 0** — durable identity / north star. Operational next actions live in Orchestrator; seasonal plans in Horizon; knowledge graph here in B2.

## North Star

I design and automate systems across energy, Bitcoin, mobility and AI that scale me out of survival-mode hustle so I can explore freely without the anxiety of standing still.

## Current Reality

Multi-source hustle (Lyft/GrubHub + immature rentals + other efforts) while building toward financial independence and reclaimed time, energy, and agency. The goal is to eliminate chronic anxiety of “falling behind” and free capacity for exploration of interests, purpose, and hobbies.

## Strongest Ikigai Overlap

What I love ∩ what I am unusually good at:

- Systems design
- Process optimization
- Automation
- Integrations across tools and systems toward a larger end

Less clear (and therefore areas of active discovery): what the world needs and what can sustain me at true scale. **Scaling itself is the central bottleneck.**

## Desired Use of Agency

Once time/energy/agency are reclaimed: open exploration of wants, needs, interests, hobbies, and purpose — without the compulsive need to keep grinding simply to stay in place.
